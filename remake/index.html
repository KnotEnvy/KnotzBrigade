A BRIGADE FIELD GUIDE

CATCH THE TIDE.

STEER THE COREYour bright core is your vulnerable point. Arrows / WASD move; hold Shift for precise movement. On touch, use the stick and hold FOCUS for fine steering. Gamepad: left stick, A fire, B guard, X surge.

CATCH. THEN COUNTER.Press X (or right mouse) for a 1.15-second Tideguard. Catch amber shots in its ring. Your countershot fires when the shield ends; more caught shots mean more damage. Recharge: 5.2 seconds.

SPEND YOUR SURGEKills, grazes and caught shots build resonance. At 100%, press C to clear enemy fire and amplify your weapon for six seconds.

STAY FOR ONE MORE CHAINLink kills for up to ×5 score. Couriers carry relay data and upgrades. Upgrades survive damage. Each new part repairs two hull points and saves a retry checkpoint.

BREAK THE FOUNDRYThe Broodkeeper opens its armor to release drones; countershots pierce it at any time. Leave its marked wake before it charges. The Furnace Warden has three linked conduits: destroy them for a six-second core opening. Red vents fire after a warning. Pressure gates block shots: fly through their mint opening, or use Guard to absorb a scrape. Clean passages reward resonance and score.

MASTER THE FLIGHTS+ requires a clean clear, all 3 relays, 12 catches and 65,000 points. Records are separate for each rig and difficulty. Boss reinforcements and defensive score have fixed budgets; fast boss clears earn more.

TAKE THE FLIGHT YOU WANTAuto-fire starts on; turn it off to use Space. P / Escape pauses. M mutes. F opens fullscreen. Cadet is forgiving, Pilot is the intended run, Ace is faster. Checkpoint retries are practice; full-run records stay separate.
