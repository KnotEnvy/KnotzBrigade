## Remaining objections, ranked by impact

1. **A heard mix is the remaining strict-gate issue.** Listen through ordinary sustained fire, Guard/counter, a warning under explosions, core opening, overload, victory and defeat at normal and high settings. Judge cue priority, comfortable loudness and whether the authored progression earns its finale. Change balances or composition in response to what is heard. More tracks, more signal metrics or another source-only scorecard cannot resolve this perceptual uncertainty. This is missing quality evidence, not a confirmed audio bug.
2. **Validate the delivery envelope on actual target hardware.** Run a full desktop and physical-phone flight, including simultaneous steering/Focus/Guard. Inspect startup and automatic resize as well as both bosses. The long software frame and accumulated clock difference justify focused observation, but their cause should be measured before changing timing or controls. Current sustained data is a significant improvement over short fixtures; it cannot certify every GPU, phone or browser.
3. **Observe a first-time play and a retry.** Check whether the player understands catching, armored/open states and the optional relay, and whether the debrief produces a concrete next attempt. Use that feedback to select any further encounter or instruction changes. This is validation of the intended appeal, not a demand for a campaign or an allegation that the game is unfun.

**No confirmed remaining gameplay or source defect was found in this round.** The observed governor defect was corrected and the affected full desktop flight rerun before scoring. I would retain the distinct bosses, authored gate/mine combinations, bounded scoring and practice flow. Under the requested R-Type/Gradius stage-craft standard, this mission now makes positioning, defensive commitment and recovery matter in its own setting. That editorial assessment does not establish superiority to those games, heard audio polish, or human-tested addictiveness.
