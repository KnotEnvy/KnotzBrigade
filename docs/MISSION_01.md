# Mission 01 — The Drowned Foundry

The intended flight is roughly three to four minutes with two distinct boss encounters. This is one complete mission, not a campaign. Its central decision is **catch → align → counter**: Tideguard protects for 1.15 seconds, turns caught amber shots into a stronger return shot, then recharges for 5.2 seconds. Ordinary weapons remain useful throughout. Surge clears bullets/vent hazards and amplifies fire for six seconds; spending it early trades safety now against a future exposed core.

| Part | Encounter purpose | Payoff |

| 01 · The Breakwater | Sine formations, aimed anglers, warning-line divers, first courier, first pressure gate and crossing escorts | Reach the gates; +2 hull, upgrade, checkpoint |

## Enemy jobs

Drones form synchronized sine/crossing paths. Stalkers cross faster. Anglers hold the firing lane and aim a three-shot spread. Razorfin divers mark an approach before committing to the player's sampled lane. Moonfish lay destructible pearls that bloom into slow radial fire after 2.8 seconds. Lucky couriers reward a detour: destroy the courier, then retrieve its data and power pickup. Caught shots, kills and grazes build resonance. Drops attract within a short pickup radius.

Pressure gates move left with a clearly marked mint opening. Their solid plates block both friendly and hostile shots and destroy enemies caught outside the gap, so they create temporary cover as well as a route constraint. A right-edge bracket previews the gap before the gate enters view. A clean passage awards 450 points and resonance once; Guard can protect a scrape. Part three alternates low/high gate openings around the warned vents, and a dedicated upper mine layer makes its courier an optional clearing-and-retrieval detour.

## The Broodkeeper

A catchable volley invites Tideguard; the brood hatch then opens for a three-second damage window while escorts deploy. A broad amber wake marks a locked charge lane for 1.1 seconds before the carrier lunges left. Leaving the wake creates another exposed opening. At half integrity the encounter speeds up. Visible bronze shutters slide away from a bright mint core during openings; resisted ordinary fire produces amber arcs. Countershots pierce armor at any time; normal fire does reduced chip damage while the hatch is shut. There is no absolute invulnerability gate.

## The Furnace Warden

Three visible linked conduit targets orbit ahead of its armored body. Destroying all three exposes the heart for 6.5 seconds, after which surviving machinery restores the links. The Warden alternates a slow curtain with safe gaps and a catchable aimed centre volley, destructible mines, and warned vent lanes. Overload adds a second vent and faster conduits. Cyan/mint marks the open core and links; red marks vents. This encounter asks the player to change targets and read safe water.

## Fairness and mastery

Movement is immediate with normalized diagonals and a slower focus mode. The player's physical vulnerable point is smaller than its sprite; a bright marker remains visible on small screens. Hits give 1.6 seconds of protection and clear nearby hostile shots. Damage preserves upgrades. New parts repair two hull and enable a practice retry. Cadet has more hull and slower/lighter enemies; Ace has less hull and faster/heavier enemies.

Chains reach ×5 and tolerate brief gaps. Boss fights preserve an existing chain while presenting targets. Each boss has only 20 score-bearing reinforcement spawns, 32 score-bearing catches and 24 score-bearing grazes; abilities continue working after those limits. A quick-clear bonus starts at 4,000 and decays by 65 per elapsed boss second. Stalling cannot produce unlimited score. Records are separate by difficulty and rig. Practice cannot update them.

S+ mastery requires a completed, undamaged full run, all three relays, at least 12 caught shots and 65,000 points. Lower ranks reward progress without calling every clean clear perfect. Debrief prompts identify the next missed objective. Every part also reports hull damage, relay recovery, catches, best chain and score, with a direct practice-from-part button. Practice and full-flight sector bests/clean clears are stored separately by rig and difficulty; part-one practice is explicitly excluded from full-run records. Practice grades use CLEAR, relay goals count only the chosen route, and Replay preserves its entry point.

## Evidence standard

The browser suite verifies both phases and every signature in Pilot/Lance, Pilot/Fan and Ace/Lance full flights using input only. Controlled fixtures cover collision/death resolution, guard/counter, invulnerability, score budgets, pause, repeated restarts, practice records and touch layouts. Those checks establish implementation and reachability. Critic judgments are subjective assessments; no automated score establishes human enjoyment, a clinical claim of addictiveness, or 60fps on untested physical devices.
